About

Queries GlyGen and UniProt APIs in real time. Retrieves glycosylation, phosphorylation, acetylation, ubiquitination, and other PTMs plus disease associations, pathways, SNVs, and PTM crosstalk data.

Data sources: UniProtKB, iPTMnet, BioMuta, UniCarbKB, PubMed.
