Strategy Summary

This tool combines 280 PTM sites across 16 neurodegeneration-associated proteins (from GlyGen/CFDE) with findings from two key mass spectrometry studies of tau PTMs in Alzheimer's and tauopathies. The goal: identify which disease-altered PTMs are most actionable for wet-lab validation and therapeutic development.

Prioritization Criteria

Targets are scored based on: (1) strong experimental evidence from GlyGen, (2) disease-specificity from the literature (Wesseling 2020, Ngoie Zola 2023), (3) presence of nearby pathogenic variants, (4) druggability of the modifying enzyme. High-priority targets have convergent evidence from multiple sources.

Variant-PTM Proximity Analysis

Missense variants within 5 residues of a PTM site may disrupt modification, creating or abolishing regulatory events. These are high-value candidates for functional studies -- a single amino acid change near a glycosylation or phosphorylation site can alter protein folding, aggregation, or signaling.
