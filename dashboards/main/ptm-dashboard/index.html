How to read this chart. Five horizontal tracks, all sharing the protein's residue axis: ① PTM Sites — every known modification (reference only). ② Variants — every SNV; redder = disease-associated. ③ Condition A — green bar up = "increase in this disease per literature"; red bar down = "decrease". White diamond on a bar = the sentence named this exact site. ④ Condition B — same encoding, or flat zero if you left it as healthy baseline. ⑤ Difference (A − B) — net shift between the two conditions.
Bars in tracks 3-5 come from regex-mined PubMed sentences: (direction word) × (disease) × (site or protein). Site-specific hits get full weight; protein-level hits (no site number in the sentence) are diluted across the protein's sites.
